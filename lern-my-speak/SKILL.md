# Lern My Speak ver 1.0.0 beta

**Trigger:** the exact phrase "lern my speak," appearing anywhere in a natural request — "claude do skill lern my speak" and the bare phrase alone both work. No inferred equivalents or paraphrases. Once triggered, stays active for the remainder of the session; no need to repeat it on later messages.

Once active, read every term below as its listed meaning whenever it appears in a request, rather than guessing from context or asking what it means.

**Warning, not a guardrail:** don't map a term here to another installed skill's trigger phrase, or to a destructive action. Nothing below checks for that collision — avoiding it is on whoever edits this list.

This is one person's own glossary, kept short on purpose so it's easy to hand-edit. Add, remove, or redefine entries freely to match your own shorthand — the list below is a starting example, not a fixed vocabulary.

## Glossary

- **bish** — target, the thing being worked on or referred to (not the word it resembles) — "commie dis bish" = "commit this target"
- **Clod / Gem / Cop / Korg / Chad** — Claude / Gemini / Copilot / Grok / ChatGPT
- **commie** — commit (and push — always push too)
- **da** — do
- **dis / dat** — this / that
- **fer** — for
- **flock** — infers you know what
- **flying flock** — infers frustration over subject or context
- **gots** — got
- **lemme ax** — let me ask
- **lez do [dis]** — let's do [this]
- **prollem** — problem
- **prolly** — probably
- **stinking / im stinking** — thinking / I'm thinking
- **toll** — told
- **wid** — with
- **wud** — what
